1. Balut

Balut is a popular Filipino street food made from a fertilized duck egg that's incubated and then boiled or steamed.

2. Isaw

Isaw is a popular Filipino street food made from barbecued pig or chicken intestines.

3. Kwek-Kwek

Kwek-Kwek are deep-fried quail eggs coated in a bright orange batter.

4. Fishball

Fish balls are made from fish paste and are boiled or deep-fried.

5. Betamax (Dugo)

Betamax is coagulated chicken blood that is skewered and grilled.

How to Cook Betamax

6. Adidas

Adidas is grilled chicken feet marinated in soy sauce and vinegar.

7. Palabok

Palabok is a Filipino noodle dish with shrimp sauce and toppings.

How to Cook Palabok

8. Chicken Proben

Proben is a popular Filipino street food made from deep-fried chicken proventriculus. It's also known as "chicken proben".

How to Cook Chicken Proben

9. Taho

Taho is a traditional Filipino snack or breakfast made of warm soft/silken tofu, sweet arnibal (brown sugar syrup),and sago pearls (similar to tapioca).

How to Make Taho

10. Banana Cue

Banana Cue is a beloved Filipino street food snack made from deep-fried saba bananas coated in rich, golden caramelized brown sugar, skewered on bamboo sticks for easy munching.
